The floating Surroundings card

Appears only while a background sound is playing, sitting above the music mini player as a clearly separate layer. Collapsed it is a name and a volume line. Tapped, it grows downward to reveal the other sounds already on the phone. Semi-transparent as a paper-tinted scrim at 88 percent with a hairline, so what is behind ghosts through rather than being frosted: no blur, no glass, no gradient.

Lamplight
The same card against deep pine at 88 percent opacity. In the dark theme the translucency reads more clearly, since the covers behind it are darker than the card. Moss becomes its Lamplight variant; nothing else changes.
Build notes
Transparency, precisely. Background is the theme's own paper or lamp colour at 88 percent alpha, with a 1px border at 16 percent ink. No backdrop blur, no frosted glass, no gradient. Content behind ghosts through rather than being obscured, which is what keeps it from reading as a Material dialog.
It appears only while a sound plays. Nothing floats by default. It slides in when a sound starts and leaves when it stops. Because it appears in response to the listener's own action, it should not read as an intrusion.
Expanding grows downward from a fixed anchor. The volume line stays where it was, so a finger already on it is not displaced. Collapsed is roughly 62dp, expanded roughly 210dp.
The list is not a browser. Only recordings already on the phone, four at most, sorted by recent use. Anything longer belongs on the Surroundings tab, which is what "All recordings" opens.
Gestures. Tap anywhere to expand or collapse. Drag along the volume line to set level. Swipe the card down to stop the sound and dismiss. Long press is unused.
It never blocks the last row. Whatever list is behind it gets bottom padding equal to the card's height plus the mini player, so nothing is permanently hidden underneath.
On the player spread it does not appear at all. The Surroundings page already holds a full volume control and library, so the card would duplicate it. It shows on Shelf, Search, Lists and More only.
Accessibility. Expanded state announced, volume as a slider with pp to ff described in plain words, and every row 44dp minimum. Survives 200 percent font scale by allowing the card to grow rather than truncate.
