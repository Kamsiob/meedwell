Meedwell
Music to sit with.

A player for music you own, built around listening without words: chamber works, solo piano, Celtic strings, ambient, film and game scores.

It plays whatever is in your collection, all of it, exactly as you bought it. But this is the listening it was made for, and it does not pretend otherwise.

Meedwell
Version 1.0.0 · AGPL-3.0 · by Kamsiob
Music you own, on a shelf, in a room.

Meedwell reads a Bandcamp collection and the files already on your phone, and puts them on one shelf. It keeps no account of you, holds no server, and asks for nothing beyond the permissions it visibly needs.

It was built around instrumental listening, and it plays whatever you own. Both of those are true at once, and neither is a marketing position.

Downloading, in Lamplight
Progress stated in recordings and megabytes rather than a spinner, and the wifi-only condition named before it interrupts anyone. Uninstalled rows say plainly that they need a connection instead of failing on tap. Nothing here is an error state.

Album titles, artists, and recordist handles are invented for this grid and are not real releases or real Freesound users. Actual recording credits come from the pipeline's attribution manifest. Durations, sizes, and megabyte figures are representative of the expected library rather than measured. Where this grid and SPEC-REVISION-01 disagree, the revision governs.
