First release.

Meedwell plays your Bandcamp collection and the audio files already on your phone, on one shelf.

Albums, composers, shelves and lists. A player whose scrubber is the piece's own loudness curve. Surroundings: ambient recordings of real places, to lay under your music or play on their own. Five tone voicings named for instruments and rooms. A sleep timer that eases down. Daylight and Lamplight themes.

No account, no ads, no subscriptions, and nothing collected.
